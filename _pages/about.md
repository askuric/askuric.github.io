Hello! My name is Antun, and I am a mechatronics engineer with a PhD in robotics, specializing in human-robot physical interaction.

I'm currently working as a postdoc at ETH Zurich's [Sensory-Motor Systems Lab](https://sms.hest.ethz.ch/) with Professors [Peter Wolf](https://sms.hest.ethz.ch/the-group/team/peter-wolf.html) and [Robert Riener](https://sms.hest.ethz.ch/the-group/team/robert-riener.html). In collaboration with Balgrist University Hospital, we are studying the use of rehabilitation exoskeletons for post-injury shoulder rehabilitation. 

At the same time, I'm part of Hugging Face <img src="/images/hf-logo.png" alt="Hugging Face" style="height:20px;width:auto;"/>, where I have a dual role: an engineering role working on open-source robotics projects alongside the [Pollen Robotics](https://pollen-robotics.com/) team, and a research role exploring different aspects of sustainability in robotics.  


### My Scientific Journey

My research has evolved from industrial robotics to human-centered assistive technology. Here is a brief look at my background:

*   **Postdoc in Rehabilitation Robotics (Present):** ETH Zurich, Switzerland. Focusing on the assistive robotics domain, where exoskeletons help humans maintain or recover their physical abilities.
*   **PhD in Human-Robot Interaction:** [INRIA](https://www.inria.fr/en) (AUCTUS team), Bordeaux, France. Under the supervision of [Vincent Padois](https://people.bordeaux.inria.fr/vincent.padois/) and [David Daney](https://people.bordeaux.inria.fr/ddaney/), I developed human-centered control strategies where robots adapt in real-time to human physical abilities in shared industrial workspaces. 
*   **M.Sc. in Mechatronics and Control Theory:** [Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing (FER)](https://www.fer.unizg.hr/en), University of Zagreb. This included international internships at Siemens <small>(Princeton, USA)</small> and Bosch <small>(Stuttgart, Germany)</small>, followed by work as a research associate at FER's *Mechatronics lab*.

### Research Interests & Sustainability

My research interests lie at the intersection of robotics, control theory, computational geometry and human-robot interaction. I am particularly interested in developing human-centered control strategies that allow robots to work safely and effectively alongside humans.

Recently, I've developed a strong interest in understanding the broader (social, environmental, sustainability, etc.) impacts of technology and particularly robotics. I am fascinated by both the potential positive and negative effects our field can have on the planet and society. 

I believe that, as roboticists and engineers, we have a responsibility to consider these impacts when designing new technologies. I'm eager to explore ways we can create more sustainable robotic systems in the future. 
*If this topic interests you, you might like this blog post: [Robotics research should think more about sustainability!](https://huggingface.co/spaces/askuric/sustainability-in-robotics)*


### Hands-on Engineering & Open Source

My favorite way of learning is by hands-on experimenting and creating educational projects that allow me to test and improve my knowledge across domains. When these projects go well, I love documenting and sharing them as open-source resources for the wider community, and occasionally making a YouTube video about them 😃.  

One project I am particularly proud of is the [*Simple**FOC**library*](/portfolio/1_simplefoclibrary), which aims to demystify Field Oriented Control for the Arduino community. The project has exceeded all my expectations, growing into an active community with **1,500+ members** and **50+ contributors**. 
🔗 *[Find out more at simplefoc.com](https://simplefoc.com)*
